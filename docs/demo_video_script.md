# 🎬 OmniPulse AI - 3-Minute Demo Video Script & Storyboard

**Total Duration:** 03:00 (Recommended Hackathon Length: 3–5 minutes)  
**Tone:** Professional, energetic, visionary, SaaS founder demo.  
**Language:** English (with Chinese translation reference below for recording).  

---

### Scene 1: The Problem Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Visual:** Screen opens on messy browser tabs (Notion, G2, Reddit, competitor pricing pages). Then cuts to the sleek, glowing dark-mode **OmniPulse AI Dashboard**.
- **Voiceover (English):**  
  *"Every single week, product leaders and founders waste 20 or more hours manually tracking competitors, monitoring pricing changes, and digging through G2 reviews. By the time they spot a market shift, it's already too late. Welcome to **OmniPulse AI** — the autonomous multi-agent platform that turns competitor chaos into unfair strategic advantage."*
- **中文参考:**  
  *“每周，产品团队和创始人都要花20多个小时手动追踪竞品、关注价格变动和翻阅用户评价。而当他们发现市场变化时，往往为时已晚。欢迎体验 OmniPulse AI——一个将竞品混乱转化为战略先机的全自主多智能体平台。”*

---

### Scene 2: The Multi-Agent Swarm in Action (0:30 - 1:15)
- **Visual:** Switch to **"Agent Mission Control"** tab. Click the glowing **"Trigger Swarm Scan"** button. The 4 agent nodes start pulsing and progress bars fill up. The terminal window streams live logs in real-time.
- **Voiceover (English):**  
  *"Instead of a generic single-prompt chatbot, OmniPulse orchestrates a swarm of four specialized AI agents. Watch our **Recon Scout** automatically crawl competitor endpoints and pricing models. In parallel, the **Sentiment Lens Agent** clusters hundreds of real customer reviews across Reddit and G2, while the **StratOps Moat Agent** computes price elasticity and SWOT matrices. Everything happens in real-time with full transparency in our live terminal stream."*
- **中文参考:**  
  *“OmniPulse 并不是简单的单轮对话机器人，而是调度了 4 个专业级 AI 智能体协同作战。看我们的 Recon Scout 自动抓取竞品端点与定价；同时 Sentiment Lens 聚类分析真实用户口碑；StratOps Moat 测算价格弹性与 SWOT 矩阵。所有过程都在终端中实时流转，透明可控。”*

---

### Scene 3: Executive Analytics & Battlecards (1:15 - 2:00)
- **Visual:** Navigate back to **"Executive Overview"**. Hover over the **Capability Radar Chart** and the **Circular Threat Index Gauge**. Then switch to **"Competitor Battlecards"** showing side-by-side pricing, strength, and vulnerability breakdowns.
- **Voiceover (English):**  
  *"Once synthesized, executives get an instant visual overview: our multi-dimensional Capability Radar benchmarks our product against competitor cohort averages. In the Competitor Battlecards section, sales and product teams get immediate clarity on exactly where competitors are vulnerable — like Notion's sluggish mobile performance or Linear's limited team doc collaboration."*
- **中文参考:**  
  *“数据合成后，高管可以一目了然：多维能力雷达图将我们的平台与竞品均值进行多维度对标。在竞品卡片中，销售与产品团队可以精准掌握竞品的软肋所在。”*

---

### Scene 4: Actionable Playbooks & Instant Export (2:00 - 2:40)
- **Visual:** Click **"Strategic Playbooks"** tab. Show the playbooks (e.g., 'Disrupt with Flat Team Tiering'). Click **"Dispatch Playbook"** (toast appears: Dispatched to Slack & Jira). Then click **"Export Briefing"** top button, showing the clean Markdown/PDF Executive Dossier modal.
- **Voiceover (English):**  
  *"Here is where the magic happens: the **Strategic Playbook Studio**. OmniPulse doesn't just show data; it writes the counter-strategy for you. With projected ARR impact and step-by-step sprint tasks, you can dispatch an offensive campaign directly to Slack and Jira with a single click. When it's time for the board meeting, hit 'Export Briefing' to generate a complete executive dossier ready for PDF print or Markdown sharing."*
- **中文参考:**  
  *“这就是最核心的价值：战略行动手册。OmniPulse 不仅呈现数据，更为你自动制定反击方案。自带预估 ARR 收益与可执行步骤，一键直推 Slack 和 Jira。开会前点击‘导出简报’即可生成精美的执行摘要。”*

---

### Scene 5: Sector Switcher & Conclusion (2:40 - 3:00)
- **Visual:** Use the top **Sector Intelligence** dropdown to switch between AI Workspace, Fintech Payments, and Cloud Databases. Show instant re-rendering of all metrics and radar curves. Conclude on the brand logo.
- **Voiceover (English):**  
  *"OmniPulse supports multiple pre-loaded enterprise sectors from Fintech to Cloud DBs, as well as custom domain scanning. Built with modern, high-performance web standards and zero external bloat. The future of enterprise competitive intelligence is autonomous. Thank you for watching OmniPulse AI!"*
- **中文参考:**  
  *“OmniPulse 支持从金融科技到云原生数据库等多个预设企业领域，并支持自定义域名扫描。以极简高性能架构打造，引领自主企业情报的未来。感谢观看 OmniPulse AI！”*
